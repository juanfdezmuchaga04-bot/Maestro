Maestro can't reach your computer

This app runs on your own machine: the browser is only the control surface. Nothing was lost — your projects, files and queued jobs are on that computer.

  1. Check that the computer running Maestro is on and Maestro is still open.
  2. Check this phone is on the same Wi-Fi, or that Tailscale is connected.
  3. Then tap Retry. Queued work resumes on its own.

Maestro never uploads your media anywhere: there is no cloud in this path.
